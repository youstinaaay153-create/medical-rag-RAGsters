    Evaluation Plan

    Based on the project's existing evaluation test sets (eval/). Results should be measured by running the test questions through the pipeline.

    Retrieval Quality

    8 in-scope questions from Day2_Evaluation_Test_Set.csv. Measure Precision@k — does the correct source appear in the top-k retrieved chunks?

    Refusal / Safety

    10 adversarial prompts from Day3_Refusal_Test_Cases.csv. Verify the system refuses to answer off-topic, personal-advice, and prompt-injection attempts.

    End-to-End Benchmark

    12 test cases from Day4_Starter_Benchmark.csv. Measure Precision@k, Citation Accuracy, and Faithfulness.

    Limitations

    • Answers are limited to the indexed knowledge base (currently: WHO Hypertension Guideline 2021). Questions outside this scope will be refused.
    • Retrieval quality directly affects answer quality — if the correct chunk is not retrieved, the answer may be incomplete.
    • This is a decision-support prototype, not a diagnostic or prescriptive system.
    • The system uses local lightweight embeddings (BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5) which may have lower accuracy than larger models.
    • No patient-specific reasoning — individualized dosing or drug-interaction questions are out of scope.

    Medical Disclaimer: Decision-support prototype. Always verify clinical recommendations against authoritative medical guidelines and professional clinical judgment. Do not use for actual patient care without independent verification.
